# One take — brief

> A camera-choreography example (engine 2): one sustained move instead of cuts.

**Concept (one line):** Rayan crosses a bridge made of light while the mascot doubts it — filmed as one continuous take.
**Platform / format:** 9:16 · ~14 s · example of `keys` + `follow`, arc blends and focus pulls.

## Beats
1. Setup — two-shot, slow push-in: "You're really crossing that?" / "It's a bridge. Bridges hold." (focus pulls between speakers).
2. The walk — the camera arcs from the two-shot to behind Rayan as he steps onto the bridge, rides alongside over
   the void, and swings to a low front ¾ as he arrives (one `full` shot with `follow` and three `keys`).
3. Payoff — he looks back over his shoulder: "It's made of light." → a locked-off wide: two small figures, a lot of space.

## Camera
The back of his head while he steps onto the bridge and in the final wide is deliberate (`allow: ["back-of-head"]`).
`move.face: "path"` keeps him facing the way he walked, so the look back plays toward the camera.
