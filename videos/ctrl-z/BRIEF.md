# Ctrl+Z — brief

> Write this before episode.json, keep it short, and update it as the idea changes. It is the intent;
> episode.json and custom code are the executable direction.

**Concept (one line):** Tiny Rayan accidentally deploys to production by falling on a giant ENTER key; undoing it
needs CTRL and Z held at once — and his AI sidekick weighs nothing.
**Platform / format:** 9:16 Shorts / Reels / TikTok · target ~28–32 s
**Audience:** developers and AI-curious viewers (Friday-deploy and "AI helper" humour)

## Hook (0–2 s)
Rayan drops out of the dark onto a giant ENTER key: CLACK, dust burst, camera shake, "DEPLOYED." slams on screen,
a PagerDuty toast pops in. The viewer instantly gets "tiny guy, giant laptop, something just went very wrong".

## Beats
1. Setup — Panic line ("Did I just deploy… on a Friday?"). Claude hops in: "Relax. Just press Control-Z."
2. Escalation — Overhead: CTRL and Z are across the keyboard (callouts). Sprint. Rayan stomps Z (it goes down);
   Claude hops on CTRL… nothing. Glitch + error popup on the giant screen. "I weigh nothing. I'm literally a cloud."
   Rayan points at the rubber debugging duck: "Duck. Now." Carries it over, drops it on CTRL — it goes down
   (the duck is heavier than the AI). Back to Z, stomp: UNDO SUCCESSFUL.
3. Twist / payoff — Chat bubble + line from Claude: "Also… I redeployed it for you." Rayan facepalms. Record
   scratch, dip to black, end title.

## Characters & performance
- Rayan: physical comedy — falling, sprinting, stomping, carrying a duck that's half his height; panic → resolve →
  despair. Readable faces in closes.
- Claude: cheerful, helpful, useless at the physical task; hops instead of pressing; proud at the end.

## Setting & look
Environment: `micro-keyboard` (giant backlit laptop at night, 14 m screen behind). Mood `macro-warm` with shallow
focus, dust in the light. Screen tells the story (deploy → error popup → success). Quick cuts (≤ 3 s), low macro
angles across the key tops, one overhead to show geography, inserts on the screen.

## Sound
Eleven v4 voices with tags ([panicked], [sighs], [proudly]). Local CC0 SFX: heavy key clack + impact on the landing,
key clicks on presses, boing on Claude's useless hops, error buzz, duck squeak, success chime, record scratch on the
payoff. No music (lets the clacks land).

## Reuse vs custom
Reused: micro-keyboard environment, recipes (press, point, pick-up, place, react), fx (impact, shake, punch-text,
toast, callout, glitch, chat, dip), screens (deploy + popup), sfx kit. Custom: only the "undo successful" screen text.

## Payoff check
Twist recontextualises the whole effort (the AI undid the undo). Last image: Rayan's facepalm on a tiny key under a
giant "DEPLOYED" screen.
